info_outline Important Information

BahaSumbong allows approved residents to report flooding and drainage problems and monitor their report statuses.

Reported information depends on resident submissions and may not have been independently verified.

BahaSumbong is an academic demonstration system. It does not predict floods or replace official disaster monitoring and emergency response services.
