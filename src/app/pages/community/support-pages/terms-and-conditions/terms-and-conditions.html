verified_user Responsible Community Reporting

Residents are encouraged to submit truthful, relevant, and accurate flooding or drainage information.

Submitting misleading reports may reduce the usefulness of community reporting information.

Report information should not be treated as an official professional assessment.

lock_outline Protect Your Information

Keep your account credentials private and avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in reports or support requests.

For this academic demonstration, use appropriate sample information instead of uploading actual identity documents or other confidential records.
