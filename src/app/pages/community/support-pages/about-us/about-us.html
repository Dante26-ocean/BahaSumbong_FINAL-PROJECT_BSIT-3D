account_tree How BahaSumbong Works

  1. Resident Account Verification

    Residents sign in using Google and complete account verification. An administrator reviews their verification request.

  2. Community Report Submission

    Approved residents select a location, identify the flooding or drainage problem, describe the concern, and attach supporting photos.

  3. Administrator Report Review

    Authorized administrators examine submitted reports and their supporting information.

  4. Report Status Updates

    Administrators can update report statuses based on their review. Residents can check the updated statuses through My Reports and Notifications.

  5. Community Mapping

    Reports with valid geographic coordinates can be displayed on the interactive map.

security Account and Report Security

BahaSumbong uses account authentication and resident verification to control access to community features.

Residents can access their own submitted reports, while authorized administrators can manage community reporting information.

Supporting report photos use protected access through the application's backend.

Users should avoid entering passwords, government identification numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information in report descriptions and support requests.

school Academic Demonstration Project

BahaSumbong is developed for academic presentation and demonstration purposes.

It is not an officially deployed government reporting platform. It does not provide official emergency announcements, flood predictions, or disaster response services.

During actual emergencies, residents should follow information and instructions from appropriate local government and emergency response authorities.
